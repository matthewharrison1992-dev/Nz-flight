Gamepad

Left stick flies, right stick yaws, triggers set throttle. A gear, X / LB flaps, B brakes, Y camera, RB autopilot, Start pause.

Take-off: throttle to the top, keep straight with RUD, drag the stick down gently at the R mark on the speed tape, then tap GEAR. To land, follow the magenta diamonds and the PAPI lights beside the runway: two white and two red means you are on the glide path. Tap Menu to change aircraft or airport.

Take-off: full throttle (9), keep straight with QE, ease back with S at the R mark on the speed tape, then G for gear up. To land, follow the magenta approach diamonds and the PAPI lights: two white, two red is on slope.
